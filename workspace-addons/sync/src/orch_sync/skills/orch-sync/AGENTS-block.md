## orch-sync
Keeps a copy of the tickets and their files in the background (never secrets or private files). Read `.agents/skills/orch-sync/SKILL.md`.
Check it: `orch sync status --json`. Sync once now: `orch sync now --json`.
Choices sync asks for (resolve, approve, take-remote, keep-key, deleted) are the person's: they need the person's proof. Ask the person; never run them yourself.
Do not start, stop or change sync unless the person asks.
