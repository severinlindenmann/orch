Tell teammates about orch

Adds a short note to your project’s guide file so your teammates’ helpers know orch is used here.

Example: a colleague’s helper learns how to open your tickets.

Let helpers finish tickets

A helper may mark its own ticket Done. When this is off it asks you to check first.

Example: the helper fixes a typo and closes the ticket; you find it under Done.

Several helpers at once

Each helper works in its own copy of your folder, so two helpers never change the same file.

Example: one helper writes the newsletter while another fixes the website.
