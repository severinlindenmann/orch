What changed after the challenge

  • Quick look no longer passes by itself. “OK all” comes only after the list is shown, one line each. Passing by itself exists only as a rule you made, shown with its name (orch dev team only).
  • Every card starts with the decision and what happens if you wait, then the one fact that decides it, always open.
  • “Where were we?” names the day and the place you said something, with a link, and warns when it may be out of date.
  • Every card says why it is in its group. “Needs more time” moves it to Take your time.
  • Take your time cards fill no answer and ask one small check question first.
  • A round is at most 5 cards, the ones that hold up work first, cards of one project together.
  • Every answer can be undone on its own; Later asks when to remind you; sending back takes your own words.
  • A second person who must also say yes is a line on the card. A condition (“waits until: tests green”) is shown and the early button is closed.
  • Results that only passed a check are not cards: they sit in one line, “Checked by the Reviewer”.
  • Ideas can be sorted in part: “Save, 2 left for later”.
  • Practice cards (a planted mistake) are off and only a setting row; no such card is in the rounds.
  • The groups are named Quick look, Short decision and Take your time, because “Decide” is already a family of card kinds.
