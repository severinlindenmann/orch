Record a call

Your microphone and the call’s sound, written down onto a meeting in Personal.

Mockup: the small window Record opens from Meetings in Personal (ORCH-1104). One step at a time in the product; all steps below, top to bottom.

1 · Before recording

Ask everyone on the call first. Record only when all of them agreed.

Next your browser asks what to share: pick the call’s tab or your whole screen, and turn on its sound. Only the sound is recorded.

2 · The browser asks what to share

Pick the call in your browser’s window

Choose the tab with the call, or your whole screen, and turn on “Share audio”.

2b · No sound in what was shared

The call’s sound is not shared

Your browser gave no sound with what you picked, so only your own voice would be recorded. A browser tab’s sound always works.

Join the call in Teams on the web, then pick its tab and turn on “Also share tab audio”. Open Teams in the browser.
