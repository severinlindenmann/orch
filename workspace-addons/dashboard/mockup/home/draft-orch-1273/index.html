ORCH-1273 · three drafts to pick from

The workspace switcher with Saved places

Each draft is a whole dashboard page with the switcher open. Everything is clickable; nothing is saved and no place really opens. The "Preview" pill at the bottom right jumps between the states. Make the window narrow (390) to see the phone.

Draft A: everything in the one menu

The menu you know, with two parts: On this computer and Saved places. It shows one thing at a time: Add place, Share my places and the keep question each replace the list and have a back arrow. New places from a link show as one line with Review, the list stays under it. "…" opens beside its row.

Trade-off: the fewest steps and nothing new to learn, and no window over the page. But the menu gets long with many places and it holds both switching and managing.

Draft B: short menu, managing in a sheet

The menu only switches: this computer and the four places you opened last, in small rows. "All saved places" opens a sheet on the right with every place and its full address, Add place, rename, remove, sharing and the keep question.

Trade-off: the menu stays short and calm however many places you save, but adding or changing a place is one step further away, and a place you rarely open is in the sheet, not in the menu.

Draft C: one panel, find first

The switcher opens a panel in the middle of the page (from the bottom on a phone). On top one field: type to find a place, or paste an address to add it. Relay and publish sit as two tiles of their own, the workspaces are a list with their address beside the name.

Trade-off: the most room, and quick with many places, because you type two letters and click. But it covers the page and looks least like the menu of today.

The same in all three

One click on a row switches. A row shows its badge, name, kind and one grey line: "last opened 3 days ago", "Sign in once" for a place this browser never signed in to, "Only in this browser" for an address on this computer, and a small "not secure" mark for an http address. Relay and publish have their own small picture. Remove has no question: it shows Undo. A place from a link is saved only after Keep, with its full address shown. Pairing a phone is not part of this.
