Ticket chats

Team chat step 1 (ORCH-1114, draft picked in ORCH-1098). Every ticket's chat in one list on Chats, newest first, next to the workspace chats. Ticket chats replace the topic list of the workspace screen (owner 21 Nov): the workspace screen keeps only its own chat (Your helper) and New topic. A ticket chat shows who wrote each message, the helper's questions as cards and the box to write. The same messages show in the Chats page on the computer. Only you and your helpers in this step; adding workspaces or people comes later.
