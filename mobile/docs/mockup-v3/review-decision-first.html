Review card: the decision first

ORCH-2187 (Focused review F). When a workspace has turned on “decision first” (orch config set workflow.decision_first on), its cards on the phone lead the same way as on the computer: You decide, then If you wait, then one What matters box. A workspace that has not turned it on sends nothing new and its cards look as before. The card for a finished result is called Test results.

The card for a ticket's finished result is named “Test results” (before: “Result”), in the chip and in the count under Review. It leads with the result, so it has no “You decide” line.

Before

Until ORCH-2187 the phone's card always led with the ticket or card title and put a plain question's own text last, as “If it looks good: …”. “If you wait” and “What matters” were only on the computer.
