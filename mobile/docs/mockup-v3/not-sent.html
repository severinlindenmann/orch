Not sent

ORCH-1942 (UX round three, draft 3). A message the server refused stays in the chat with Not sent, one short sentence that says why (when the phone knows) and what to do, and Retry / Discard. The server's own words never show. VoiceOver reads “Not sent” and the sentence once, as one line. Waiting offline is not a failure: it keeps the clock.

Before

Until ORCH-1942 the bubble said only “Not sent” with Retry / Discard. The server's sentence was a VoiceOver hint and also became the error line on Chats, so it could say things like “This phone may not reach that workspace.” and vanished with that line.
